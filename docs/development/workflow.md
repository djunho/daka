---

# Pull Requests

Approval Policy:

- Self-approval of Pull Requests is strictly prohibited.

- All Pull Requests require review and approval from at least one peer on the team.

- Reviews should ideally be assigned to the team member whose domain knowledge is most relevant to the changes introduced.

- In cases of urgency or unavailability of the preferred reviewer, approval may be granted by any other team member.

---

# Code Review

Reviewing:

- Approval of a Pull Request is contingent upon a thorough code review of the proposed changes. The reviewer must have a clear understanding of both the nature of the changes and the rationale behind them before granting approval.
